Unlocking Tomorrow: How AI and Deep Learning Shape Our World

A guest lecture on the growing impact of AI and Deep Learning in shaping future technologies and industries.

In association with the Department of ECE, RIT Chennai Completed

About the Event

"Unlocking Tomorrow: How AI and Deep Learning Shape Our World" was an insightful guest lecture organized by the ACM RIT Student Chapter in association with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology. The event focused on the growing impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Deep Learning and their role in shaping future technologies and industries.

The session featured Mr. Suresh R. Rajagopal, Director – Global Technology & AI Transformation, as the resource person. He shared valuable insights into emerging AI technologies, real-world applications, industry trends, and future opportunities for students in the field of AI and Deep Learning.

The lecture was actively attended by members of the ACM RIT Student Chapter and students from the ECE Department, providing participants with an opportunity to gain industry-oriented knowledge and understand the evolving landscape of AI.
