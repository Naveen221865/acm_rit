Who We Are

About ACM RIT

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) is the world's largest computing society. Our student chapter at RIT brings that global community to your campus.

🌐

What is ACM?

ACM is the world's largest educational and scientific computing society, uniting educators, researchers, and professionals to inspire dialogue, share resources, and address the field's challenges.

🏫

Why This Chapter?

We exist to build a strong computing culture at RIT — connecting students with opportunities, mentors, and a community that helps them grow beyond the classroom.
