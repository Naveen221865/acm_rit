Global Organization

What is ACM?

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) is the world's largest educational and scientific computing society, founded in 1947.

With over 100,000 members across 190 countries, ACM delivers resources that advance computing as a science and a profession — through publications, conferences, and student chapters worldwide.

ACM Student Chapters are the grassroots of this global community, giving students direct access to a network of professionals, researchers, and innovators.

Our Chapter

ACM RIT Student Chapter

We are the official ACM Student Chapter at Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology, Chennai. Our chapter was established to bring the global ACM community to our campus.

We are a student-run organization open to all students at RIT — regardless of department or skill level. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced coder, there's a place for you here.

Our chapter organizes workshops, hackathons, coding sessions, and networking events to help students grow technically and professionally.
